YVGESC1 - Custom BLDC Driver

Designed in KiCad and fabricated with JLCPCB for driving my custom motors and drivetrains. Check out the full documentations page below for instructions, design process, schematics, and actual details. Features USB flashing, as well as a USB to CAN pipeline, and a heat dissipation algorithm without a brake resistor.

  • Max Bus Voltage/Continuous/Peak Current/ 50V/30A/52A
  • Gate Driver/MCU DRV8323H/STM32G431RBT6
  • Flashing ST-Link SWD, USB
  • Communications CAN, USB to SLCAN
  • Regen Firmware-controlled Motor Coil Dissipation
  • Encoder AS5047P Absolute Magnetic
  • Status Manufactured, writing firmware
